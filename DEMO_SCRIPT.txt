LANDVERSE 3D — Live Demo Script (10 min)
==========================================

PREREQUISITES:
  Terminal 1: start-backend.bat   (API on :8000)
  Terminal 2: start-frontend.bat  (UI on :5173)
  Browser:    http://localhost:5173


STEP 1 — Landing Page (30 sec)
-------------------------------
URL: http://localhost:5173/

Say: "This is LANDVERSE 3D — an AI-powered 3D property intelligence platform.
It detects buildings from satellite imagery, assigns India's ULPIN property IDs,
validates records, and renders everything in an interactive 3D city."

Show: 6 feature cards, tech stack badges, quick start block.
Point: "Backend, Frontend, 3D — all in one repo."


STEP 2 — Dashboard (45 sec)
-------------------------------
URL: http://localhost:5173/dashboard

Say: "The dashboard streams live stats from the database."

Highlight:
  - 5 Parcels, 8 Buildings, 1 ULPIN, 5 Infra assets
  - 92.4% average AI confidence — very high
  - 35m average building height, 11,800 sq.m tracked
  - Bar chart: building types (Residential High/Mid/Low, Commercial, Mixed)
  - Pie chart: 100% buildings pass validation
  - Recent jobs panel (empty until you run AI analysis)


STEP 3 — 3D City Map (2 min) ★ HERO MOMENT
-------------------------------
URL: http://localhost:5173/map

Say: "This is the core — an interactive 3D city rendered from database records."

Actions:
  1. Drag to orbit the city — "You can rotate around the entire city"
  2. Scroll to zoom — "Fly in close to any building"
  3. Hover a building — "See the code, height, floors, type, AI confidence"
  4. Click BLD-2041 — Drill-down panel opens on the right
     - Parcel: PRC-01928, Hyderabad, 2500.5 sq.m
     - Building: BLD-2041, 42.6m, 12 floors, Residential High Rise
     - Floor 4 expanded → 4 units: 401 (Owned), 402 (Owned), 403 (Rented), 404 (Owned)
     - "Full Parcel → Building → Floor → Unit hierarchy"
  5. Type "Tower" in the filter — "Only Commercial Towers shown"
  6. Explain: "Each tower is procedurally generated from real height, floors, and footprint polygon"

Say: "No other tool in India gives you this Parcel-to-Unit 3D drill-down."


STEP 4 — Explode + Underground (1 min)
-------------------------------
Same page: http://localhost:5173/map

Actions:
  1. Drag the Explode slider → floor slabs separate vertically
     - "You can see individual floor plates separating"
  2. Click Underground toggle → pipes appear below the city
     - Blue = Water Pipeline (8.4m)
     - Yellow = Electric Cable (5.0m)
     - Grey = Sewer (10.2m)
     - Pink = Metro Tunnel (25.0m)
     - Green = Fiber Cable (3.5m)
  3. "The soil slice shows where the ground ends and infrastructure begins"

Say: "We don't stop at the surface — underground awareness is critical for construction."


STEP 5 — AI Analysis (2 min) ★ WOW MOMENT
-------------------------------
URL: http://localhost:5173/analysis

Say: "This is where AI meets property intelligence."

Actions:
  1. Click the upload area → choose any building photo (JPG/PNG)
     - "Upload a drone or satellite image — any building photo works"
  2. Click Upload → file uploaded, size shown
  3. Select a parcel from dropdown (or leave empty)
  4. Check "auto-create building"
  5. Click "Run AI analysis"
  6. Result appears:
     - Building type, confidence (e.g. 98.5%), height, floors
     - Annotated overlay image (green detection boundary drawn on the photo)
     - Footprint polygon (normalized 0-1 coordinates)
     - "Created building BLD-2101" — now visible in 3D Map

Say: "Real OpenCV — no mock data. Canny edge detection, contour finding,
polygon extraction. The footprint is actual geometry you can use in 3D."

Optional: Go back to /map → show the newly created building in 3D.


STEP 6 — ULPIN (1 min)
-------------------------------
URL: http://localhost:5173/ulpin

Actions:
  1. Show the form: IND / TG / HYD / 01928 / Building 1 / Floor 4 / Unit 1
  2. Point to the live preview: "IND-TG-HYD-01928-B01-F04-U01"
  3. Click "Generate & register" → ULPIN registered with green confirmation
  4. In the Validate panel, paste: IND-TG-HYD-01928-B01-F04-U01
  5. Click Check → "Valid ULPIN" with all 7 parts parsed
  6. Show the registry list: 2 ULPINs now registered

Say: "India's ULPIN system needs tools like this. We generate, validate, and
store ULPINs — all interactive, all in 3D context."


STEP 7 — Validation (1 min)
-------------------------------
URL: http://localhost:5173/validation

Actions:
  1. Select BLD-2043 (Commercial Tower, 70m, 20 floors)
  2. Click "Run validation"
  3. Result: Score 100, Status VALID
  4. Show checks: height_per_floor = 3.50 ✓, confidence = 96.8% ✓
  5. "Zero warnings — this building passes all 6 rule checks"
  6. Show history: 2 validation records now stored

Say: "Our 6-rule engine catches inconsistencies that manual surveys miss.
Height-to-floor ratio, AI confidence bands, realistic ranges."


STEP 8 — Infrastructure + Conflict (1 min)
-------------------------------
URL: http://localhost:5173/infrastructure

Actions:
  1. Show the 3D underground view again (pipes visible)
  2. Show the 5 registered assets with types, depths, owners
  3. In the Conflict Check panel:
     - Foundation depth: 8m, Tolerance: 3m
  4. Click "Check conflicts"
  5. Result: 3 conflicts found
     - Water Pipeline at 8.4m → gap 0.4m → HIGH severity
     - Electric Cable at 5.0m → gap 3.0m → MEDIUM severity
     - Sewer at 10.2m → gap 2.2m → MEDIUM severity

Say: "Before you dig, check what's below. This saves lives and millions in
damage. No other property tool does this."
